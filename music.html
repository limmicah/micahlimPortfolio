Original Music

Connection

Connection is about faith as a deliberate choice. It reflects the desire to see and understand the goodness of God, yet it also acknowledges that faith does not depend on perfect clarity. Even when God feels distant or unseen, the piece points to the decision to continue praising and trusting Him. Faith is not a passive state of mind, but an active pursuit—one that trusts and perseveres.

Alto sax: Micah Lim. Tenor sax: Dontae James. Keys: Linus Tsang. Drums: Ben Sachar. Bass: Claudia Persinger. Flute: Teddy Morris. Clarinet: Karolina Yoo. Cello: Vera Lim. Viola: Olivia Koumpouras. Violins: Dylan Lai, Ashley Peng. Vocals: Kellen Rinehart

Faith

Faith is inspired by the experience of faith itself. While faith is not merely an emotion, this piece attempts to capture the beauty of those moments when God's goodness becomes deeply tangible. Such moments can bring peace and assurance, especially in seasons of darkness. It is a reflection on the comfort and hope found in knowing that God is good.

Alto sax: Micah Lim. Tenor sax: Dontae James. Keys: Linus Tsang. Drums: Ben Sachar. Bass: Claudia Persinger. Flute: Teddy Morris. Clarinet: Karolina Yoo. Cello: Vera Lim. Viola: Olivia Koumpouras. Violins: Dylan Lai, Ashley Peng. Vocals: Kellen Rinehart

Fantasy in C.

A fantasia (or fantasy) is a free-form musical composition that does not adhere to strict structural conventions. Reflecting this idea, Fantasy in C. unfolds as a musical train of thought. It begins with a bright fanfare and gradually moves through shifting colors and territories, at times moving jaggedly and at others flowing smoothly. The music frequently changes direction, altering its perspective throughout the piece, and is filled with both unexpected turns and subtle, reflective moments.

Wind from Heaven

Written for full orchestra, Wind from Heaven is a joyous fanfare which follows a single harmonic motif through a series of meters and colors.
